Academic Viva Architecture: 4 Members & 8 Relational Tables

Every team member manages exactly 2 database tables and owns a major OOP and DBMS concept to present to the faculty:

Member 1: StudentModule.java

Tables: 1. users & 2. students

OOP: Inheritance (User base & Student subclass) and Encapsulation with private attributes.

DBMS: Student registration and login queries using PreparedStatement.

Member 2: RoomModule.java

Tables: 3. hostel_blocks & 4. rooms

OOP: Abstraction (abstract Room) and Polymorphism (StandardRoom, DeluxeRoom, ACRoom overriding calculateRent()).

DBMS: Room inventory, vacancy queries, and method overloading (search by capacity vs type).

Member 3: AllocationModule.java

Tables: 5. wardens & 6. allocations

OOP: Domain composition and business rule validation.

DBMS: ACID Transactions using setAutoCommit(false), atomicity across allocation and bed vacancy decrement, commit/rollback.

Member 4: BillingAndComplaintModule.java

Tables: 7. fee_payments & 8. complaints

OOP: Dynamic tariff integration (invoking polymorphic room rent) and ticket state lifecycle.

DBMS: SQL Aggregations (SUM(amount)) for fee balance, multi-table tracking, and complaint status updates.
